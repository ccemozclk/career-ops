## C) Level & Strategy

### Detected level vs natural fit

The JD title is **"Engineer"** (not "Senior Engineer", not "Specialist", not "Lead") and the experience bar is **"Minimum 2+ years"**. This skews the role toward mid-level, NOT senior. Cem is at **4 years 10 months** with senior-shaped scope (full-lifecycle ML, production deployment, supply chain pipeline ownership). There is a real downlevel risk where the offer comes back below his target compensation band.

However, the **scope described** (statistical models + applied ML + data quality governance + cross-functional alignment + contributing to analytics platforms + technical documentation + external-partner liaison) is squarely senior-IC work. Ford Otosan likely has a title ladder (Engineer → Senior Engineer → Lead → Manager) and this is the first or second rung.

### Plan A — "Sell senior without lying"

- **Frame the Birinci predictive maintenance work as the exact prototype** of what they need on connected trucks. Both are: high-frequency sensor data + asymmetric failure cost + adoption challenge with on-the-ground operators. The asset moved from a stamping press to a heavy-duty truck — the data engineering and ML discipline transfer 1:1.
- **Anchor on "I'm not just an analyst — I own the path from raw sensor data to a containerized production service"** (Anadolu Django ML platform + Credit Risk MLOps). This is what separates a senior IC from a 2-year data analyst.
- **Lead with the operations adoption story (Story 1, _profile.md article-digest):** "The model was the easiest part. The hardest 70% was getting maintenance crews to trust alerts that came from the computer." That single sentence in an interview signals seniority in an industrial setting.
- Use the **Industrial Engineering / cost-function angle** as the differentiator — "before I model, I ask what it costs when the model is wrong, in each direction" (FN = missed prognostic = roadside breakdown of a customer's truck; FP = unnecessary service stop = revenue impact and trust erosion).

### Plan B — "If they downlevel me"

Acceptance terms (in order of importance):
1. **Title agreement to revisit at 6 months** based on scope delivery (predictive maintenance pipeline owned end-to-end + at least one prognostics use case live).
2. **Comp at the top of the Engineer band, not the middle** — i.e., comp aligned with the candidate's actual experience, not the bar in the JD.
3. **Clear documented criteria for Senior promotion** — written, not verbal.
4. If 1-3 aren't possible: walk. Ford Otosan brand + automotive domain match are valuable but not at a 20%+ comp cut vs target.
