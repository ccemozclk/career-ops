We’re looking for a Senior Data Analyst;

In this role, you will work closely with business and product teams to support data-driven decision-making by analyzing large-scale e-commerce data and generating actionable insights. You will evaluate merchant performance, manage KPI tracking and dashboards, and translate complex analyses into clear business insights for stakeholders.

You will collaborate with Technology Product Teams, BI Developers, and Data Engineers to deliver both fast analytical solutions for immediate business needs and scalable, high-quality analytics solutions that create long-term impact across the organization.



Responsibilities:

Act as a strategic data partner for business and product teams by identifying key problems and driving data-driven solutions.
Analyze large-scale e-commerce data to generate insights that inform business decisions and improve performance.
Evaluate merchant behavior and performance across business and product touchpoints to identify growth opportunities and risks.
Develop and maintain data models, dashboards, and analytical outputs to track KPIs and enable visibility.
Translate complex analyses into clear, actionable insights for technical and non-technical stakeholders.
Collaborate cross-functionally to execute data initiatives and ensure measurable impact.
Ensure data quality and apply best practices to maintain a reliable analytics environment.


Basic Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in a quantitative or relevant field.
4+ years of experience in data analytics or business intelligence.
Strong SQL skills and experience with large, complex datasets.
Solid understanding of statistical concepts and their application in business and product contexts.
Strong analytical thinking with the ability to translate data into actionable insights.
Experience working with business and product teams in cross-functional environments.
Experience in e-commerce, marketplace, telecom, or banking is a plus.
Commercial mindset with an understanding of business dynamics.


Preferred Qualifications:

Experience in A/B testing and funnel analysis.
Experience working with big data environments (preferably BigQuery).
